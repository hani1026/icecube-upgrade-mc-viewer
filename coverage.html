Each cell combines flavor/sign × CC/NC × energy × cos(zenith) × azimuth. At most two events per cell, chosen by deterministic hash priority from 30 evenly spaced source files per dataset. This is an unweighted gallery, not a rate prediction. Empty cells mean no eligible event in the scanned pool; they do not establish a physical exclusion.

The first table groups selected events by energy; the second by cos(zenith). Each table cell has a target of 40 events, with the other direction/energy bins and azimuth sectors combined. Direction bins are equal in cos(zenith), from −1 (upgoing) to +1 (downgoing). Azimuth sectors are 0–90°, 90–180°, 180–270°, 270–360°.

All selected events passed the Queso L3/L4 booleans and at least 7 fiducial hits. Native pulses, charges, widths, PMT indices and full truth trees are retained. MuonGun and noise-only directories on the input drive contained no files.
